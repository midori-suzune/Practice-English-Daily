topic : Language

implicit : ngầm, ẩn ý (suggested or understood without being directly stated)

explicit : rõ ràng, dứt khoát (stated clearly and in detail, leaving no room for confusion or doubt)

get to a level / reach a level : đạt trình độ (to reach a certain standard or proficiency in a skill or subject, often through practice or study)

the right direction : hướng đi đúng (the correct or appropriate path or course of action, often leading to a desired outcome)

keep up with something / keep up : theo kịp (to stay at the same level or pace as something/someone, often referring to progress or development)

(a) fair share of something : một phần kha khá của cái gì (a reasonable or equitable portion of something, often referring to resources, responsibilities, or benefits)

obscure : mơ hồ, không rõ ràng (not clearly expressed or easily understood, often referring to ideas, concepts, or information)

go too far : đi quá xa, vượt quá giới hạn (to exceed acceptable limits or boundaries, often in behavior, actions, or decisions)

what's cracking? : có gì mới, có gì hay (a slang phrase used to ask about the latest news, events, or what is happening)

take a dump : đi nặng (to go to the bathroom, specifically used informally/crudely to refer to defecation)

make someone lose composure : làm ai mất bình tĩnh (to cause someone to become agitated, upset, or lose their calmness, often through provocation or unexpected events)
